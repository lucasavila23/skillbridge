# Persona: The Once-Bitten Owner

Business side — distinct from [The Guarded Owner](guarded-owner.md).

**"I already paid for a bad website. I won't get burned twice."**

**Grounding: HYPOTHESIS-HEAVY** — no primary interviews yet.

**Evidence review — 2026-09-22:** the [first two business interviews](../validation/interviews-2026-09-22.md)
(Baya, Nolita) were reviewed and **neither reports a prior bad experience
with paid digital work** — Baya's past provider was a trusted friend who
helped for free, and Nolita has paid no one. This persona remains completely
untested; it is not confirmed *or* ruled out by this round. See
[findings](../validation/findings-2026-09-22.md) and
[The Guarded Owner](guarded-owner.md), which *did* get partial grounding
from the same two interviews.

## How this differs from the Guarded Owner

The Guarded Owner has general uncertainty about trusting a student team.

The Once-Bitten Owner has a specific previous negative experience with paid
digital work — such as a freelancer, agency, or informal provider delivering
late, poor-quality, or unusable work.

Their concern is not necessarily "students can't do this." It is "I don't
want to repeat what happened last time."

## Situation / Trigger

Owns a small local business and previously paid for digital work that did
not meet expectations. A new digital need has now become important — for
example, a website redesign, booking system, or SEO cleanup — but the
previous experience makes them cautious about spending again.

## Job to be done

When I've already wasted money on digital work that didn't stick, I want a
tightly controlled way to get the next project done properly, so I don't pay
again for something I'll have to replace or fix.

## Recent behaviour — WORKING ASSUMPTION

May collect quotes and examples from providers but delay committing because
of the previous experience. May become highly involved in reviewing work or
controlling scope in an attempt to prevent another failure.

## Current alternatives — WORKING ASSUMPTION

- Patch the existing website themselves
- Delay the project
- Ask someone they already know
- Try another low-cost freelancer/provider

## Friction → consequence

Past negative experience → lower trust → greater need for control → slower
decisions and more scrutiny of deliverables.

Potential consequence: the owner delays the project altogether, continues
using an inadequate digital tool, or creates friction with the provider.

## Tool / service behaviour — HYPOTHESIS

Likely to prefer:

- Fixed deliverables
- Clear milestones
- A defined deadline
- A named person responsible for the project
- Examples of completed work
- A clear process for resolving problems

## Trust boundary — HYPOTHESIS

May be willing to work with students if there is:

- A written project charter
- Clearly defined scope
- Milestone-based payments
- Some form of refund/protection mechanism
- A clear way to stop the project if agreed expectations aren't met

A full upfront payment may be particularly difficult to accept.

## Failure threshold — UNKNOWN

Possible triggers include:

- Missed deadlines without clear communication
- Deliverables that cannot actually be used
- Repeated excuses or unclear ownership
- Discovering that the finished work requires another provider to fix it

These need to be tested rather than treated as established behaviour.

## Assumptions this persona stresses

- Business-side trust after previous negative experiences
- Willingness to pay for digital work
- Whether delivered quality is good enough to generate repeat/referral behaviour

## Product implications — HYPOTHESES

If this persona exists, it could increase the importance of:

- Milestone-based payments
- Clear project charters
- Refund/protection rules
- Named project ownership
- Before/after examples of completed work
- Evidence of successful student-business projects

## Validate next

During the 10-business outreach, ask:

> "Have you paid someone for website or digital work before? How did that experience go?"

Then compare owners with and without previous negative experiences.

For owners who report a bad experience, test whether they respond
differently to:

- Full upfront payment
- Milestone-based payment
- Milestone-based payment + refund/protection mechanism

Also test:

> "Would seeing a relevant before-and-after example from a previous project make you more comfortable working with us?"

The goal is to test whether a relevant before/after case study addresses
concerns from previously burned owners better than a generic student
profile.
