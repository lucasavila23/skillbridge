# Archive: SkillBridge v1, paid projects for small businesses

**Archived:** 2026-10-12 · **Replaced by:** the v2 challenge platform in the live
[evidence log](../../opportunity.md).

## What v1 was

> An open platform where teams of tech students take on small, real, **paid**
> "campaigns" for small businesses and come away with a **verified record of
> shipped client work**, plus some cash.

The riskiest assumption was business-side trust: will a small business hand a
real, paid project to an inexperienced student team? The planned test was a
concierge pitch to ten qualifying businesses with a 10% deposit
([v1 experiment card](experiment-card.md)).

## Why it was archived

| Evidence | What it showed | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Business test never ran | No formal pitch, signed charter or deposit was recorded | [v1 recruitment](recruitment.md) |
| Business pull is weak | Nolita's manager had no current project and said even cheap work costs time to brief and review | [interviews](../../validation/interviews-2026-09-22.md), [findings](../../validation/findings-2026-09-22.md) |
| Student time is short | 12/17 can give four hours a week or less; none said eight or more | [survey summary](../../validation/survey-summary-2026-10-12.md) |
| Contribution is the pain | 14/17 struggle to explain their personal contribution; 12/17 to show experience beyond coursework | [survey summary](../../validation/survey-summary-2026-10-12.md), [pilot assessment](../../market-research/pilot-assessment-2026-10-01.md) |
| Students want structure and feedback | Clear scope and deadlines 11/17; lack of feedback 8/17 | [survey summary](../../validation/survey-summary-2026-10-12.md) |

The student Job to Be Done did not change; the solution and the paying side did.
This is a team decision on existing evidence, not a validated result.
