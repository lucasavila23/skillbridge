# Individual-contribution verification: competitor review

**Reviewed 2026-10-13.** Public-source feature comparison, not a product demo
or real-user validation. This updates the contribution claims in the
[competitive analysis](competitive-analysis.md#v2-alternatives-challenge-and-assessment-platforms-2026-10-12).

## What we mean by the feature

**Individual-contribution verification** checks what a particular student
personally produced within a team project. A team roster, project score or
solo skills test does not by itself establish that contribution.

SkillBridge's proposed implementation connects work evidence, peer confirmation
and engineer review to a student's company-calibrated challenge certificate.
It remains a proposed capability: accuracy, reviewer effort and employer value
have not been demonstrated. Certification also requires a passing result under
the [current rules](../experiment-card.md#certification-eligibility).

## Current positioning

> SkillBridge aims to make each student's verified contribution visible in a
> company-calibrated challenge certificate. We did not find this combined
> feature in the reviewed public offers of Forage, HackerRank certifications,
> Devpost or Kaggle. NUWE does not publicly disclose an equivalent feature in
> the material reviewed.

This supports a **specific proposed difference from the four benchmark offers**,
not a claim that SkillBridge is the only platform able to verify contribution.
Riipen's partial overlap is retained in the comparison. No feature demonstration
or direct vendor confirmation was obtained.

## What changes in the idea and test

- Make the certificate show attributable work and how it was checked, rather
  than presenting team membership as verification.
- Retain peer confirmation and engineer review; do not equate commit counts
  with contribution or claim the method works before testing it.
- In the existing recruiter comparison, ask which contribution evidence
  changes their assessment and what their current alternative already gives
  them. Keep the original threshold unchanged; this explains the result.
- To establish exclusivity more strongly, inspect equivalent vendor outputs
  and clarify NUWE's offering. To establish value, observe whether recruiters
  use the contribution evidence. Desk research alone establishes neither.
