# SkillBridge Independent Market & Product Research Report

**Research cutoff date: 3 October 2026**

---

## 1. Decision Brief (≤200 words)

Pilot evidence shows student interest in paid client work and some business willingness-to-pay statements, but zero verified signed charters + deposits from the defined 10-business test, unresolved funding sources for student payments, missing denominators/cohorts, and an unexplained obstacle-table overcount. Desk research finds real demand for bounded websites/landing pages among Madrid micro/SMEs (prices €300–2,500 for simple scopes), yet strong substitutes (DIY, freelancers on Malt at €150–300+/day, agencies, postponement) and closed Kit Digital new applications. Legal pathways exist via university prácticas or autónomo but impose Social Security, tax, IP, GDPR and non-EU constraints that a student team cannot freely choose around. Delivery economics are unproven; break-even requires customer-funded prices high enough to cover student pay + coordination + QA + founder time.

**Recommendation for 2–4-week sprint:** Complete and reconcile the existing 10-business test (confirm all qualifying pitches occurred and windows closed). Do not scale outreach or product investment until ≥2 conversions or clear refusal data. Minimum resources: 15–25 founder hours + any already-budgeted student capacity. If 0–1, narrow to sole-trader/landing-page only or defer. Avoid claims of validated demand, profitable unit economics or recruiter hiring impact.

## 2. Findings

### 2.1 Business Demand and Access (Highest Priority)

Three promising initial segments (Madrid-region operational businesses with owner/GM decision-maker able to authorise payment):

#### Segment 1: Sole traders / 0–2 employees needing a single landing page or basic informative site
- **Concrete task:** Campaign or service launch page (1–5 pages, contact form, basic SEO).
- **Trigger:** New service, Google Ads/Meta campaign, or lost visibility.
- **Urgency:** Weeks.
- **Current solutions:** Wix/WordPress DIY, friends/family, low-cost freelancers (€300–900).
- **Evidence of spend:** Advertised landing prices €300–1,500; completed basic sites from €390.
- **Objections to students:** Quality risk, support after handover, timeline reliability.
- **Channels:** Local business associations, Google Business Profile searches, LinkedIn outreach, word-of-mouth.
- **Conditions to choose SkillBridge:** Price ≤€800–1,200, clear acceptance criteria, deposit + refund clause, and handover artefacts.
- **Conditions to decline:** Agency quote is comparable or DIY feels sufficient.

#### Segment 2: Micro-businesses 1–9 employees needing a 5–10 page corporate/escaparate site
- **Task:** Responsive site with contact, services, basic SEO.
- **Trigger:** Digitalisation push or competitor pressure.
- **Spend evidence:** €590–2,500 range common; higher for custom.
- **Substitutes:** Malt freelancers (€180–300/day junior–mid), small agencies, postponement.
- **Kit Digital note:** New applications closed since 31 Oct 2025 (only residual execution/justification of prior awards); does not currently subsidise new willingness-to-pay.

#### Segment 3: Service professionals (consultants, lawyers, local trades) needing campaign-specific landing
- Similar economics and objections to Segment 1; higher urgency when tied to paid ads.

**Conclusion on desk research vs commercial test:** Desk research supports price benchmarks and substitute intensity but cannot establish conversion rates, actual willingness to entrust inexperienced student teams, or sales-cycle length. Only the commercial test can do that. Advertised/quoted prices are not completed transactions; stated interest is not signed charter + deposit.

### 2.2 Legal Feasibility and Delivery Economics

#### Material requirements for a small pilot (official sources, current as of 2026)
- **University-linked prácticas** (curriculares or extracurriculares) under RD 592/2014 + DA 52ª LGSS (in force 1 Jan 2024): Mandatory Social Security affiliation (paid or unpaid). Host or university registers; unpaid flat-rate contribution ~€2.54/day (95% reduction possible). Not an employment relationship. University convenio required; IP/portfolio permissions depend on agreement.
- **Genuine employment or autónomo (RETA):** Possible for EU students and, under the 20 May 2025 Reglamento de Extranjería, non-EU higher-education students up to 30 h/week if compatible with studies; self-employment requires separate authorisation + RETA contributions (minimum ~€200+/month). Tax (IRPF) and Social Security responsibilities fall on payer or autónomo.
- **Non-EU students:** Study authorisation covers curricular prácticas; extracurricular/self-employed need extra checks.
- **Client contracts, liability, GDPR/LOPDGDD, IP assignment and portfolio rights** must be explicit. University policies vary and can restrict commercial use of student work.

**Qualified local legal advice is required before any paid pilot; classification cannot be freely chosen if the work organisation looks like employment.**

DIY tools, Malt freelancers (€180–320/day Madrid front-end), small agencies (€590–2,500+), friends/family, postponement, residual Kit Digital execution.

#### Comparison matrix (most relevant dimensions)
Effort / Price / Support / Output quality / Contribution traceability / Obligations: SkillBridge potentially differentiates on traceable student contribution + bounded student-team price, but only if delivery reliability is proven. Current evidence does not establish compelling advantage over freelancers or DIY for risk-averse owners. Riipen/Forage/Parker Dewey are primarily North-American/Canadian and not readily accessible local substitutes.

### 2.4 Student Commitment and Recruiter Value

Eligible students (software/web/data years 2–4, Madrid, limited internship/referral access) show stated interest in concrete briefs with payment and schedule (4/11 in pilot survey) and difficulty demonstrating individual contribution beyond coursework. Sustained participation depends on compensation relative to opportunity cost, clear support, realistic hours and team coordination. Unpaid programmes are comparisons, not assumed models.

Incremental value of attributable client work vs existing projects is plausible for portfolio clarity; recruiter preference studies exist internationally but transferability is limited and no Madrid-specific causal hiring-outcome data was found. Selection effects and small samples constrain strength. Keep cohorts separate; do not equate stated intention or recruiter “clearer evaluation” with hiring outcomes.

### 2.5 Market Estimate Check (≤300 words)

Official Comunidad de Madrid data for 2024/25: ~377,840 university students (public + private + UNED centres). The 260k/261.7k anchor is a lower provisional or institutional subset with campus/deduplication limitations. Tech/ICT-relevant programmes (Informática, Software, Datos, related Engineering) are a minority; exact years 2–4 headcount for target fields not published in aggregate. STEM new entrants ~15k regionally, but independence of the four rates (10% relevant × 75% years 2–4 × 50% no internship/referral × 20% ready) is an assumption.

Eligible population is smaller than 2,000 once filters are applied rigorously; reachable students via campus channels, faculty lists or student societies are a further subset. Actual readiness under a funded offer remains unmeasured. Sensitivity: if readiness is 5–10% instead of 20%, annual addressable falls to ~500–1,000. Do not derive project demand by dividing students by assumed team size.

## 3. Five Most Consequential Unresolved Assumptions
*(Ranked by decision impact × uncertainty × dependency)*

1. **Businesses will sign charter + pay 10% deposit for student teams on bounded digital work** (impact high; uncertainty high; dependency total). Resolve cost/time: finish existing test (~10–20 h).
2. **Student teams can deliver accepted work on schedule without excessive rework/refunds** (high/high/high). Resolve: deliver any secured pilots + post-mortems.
3. **Customer funding covers student pay + coordination + QA** (high/high/high). Resolve: actual P&L on first projects.
4. **Legal classification (prácticas vs autónomo/employment) is operable for pilot scale without university or Social Security blockers** (high/medium/high). Resolve: qualified local counsel + university contact (~5–10 h + fees).
5. **Eligible students sustain participation once real deadlines and client feedback appear** (medium/medium/medium). Resolve: retention data from any live campaigns.

## 4. Exactly Three Proposed Next Experiments

### Experiment 1: Complete/reconcile existing 10-business test
- **Maps to assumption:** 1
- **Recruitment:** Original 10 qualifying businesses
- **Hypothesis:** ≥2 will sign charter + pay 10% deposit within 10 days
- **Method:** Confirm all pitches sent, windows closed, non-responses retained in denominator
- **Observable action:** Signed charters + banked deposits
- **Numerator/denominator:** Conversions / 10
- **Observation window:** Current + 10 days
- **Minimum resources:** 10–15 founder hours (no new cash)
- **Proposed decision rule:** ≥2 → kick-off within 5 business days; =1 → test 5 more under same rules; 0 → stop scaling, investigate refusals
- **Inconclusive result means:** Incomplete pitch confirmation → do not score 0/10
- **Consent/privacy:** Existing offers only; no deceptive commitments. Genuine offers require actual delivery capacity and funding.

### Experiment 2: Refusal-reason interviews + micro-task probe
*(If 0–1 from Experiment 1)*
- **Maps to assumptions:** 1 & 3
- **Recruitment:** The 10 non-converters + 5 new sole-traders
- **Hypothesis:** Main objections are quality/support/timeline, not absolute refusal of student teams
- **Method:** Structured 15-min calls + optional €10 registration + 1-week micro-task (historical rule)
- **Observable action:** Coded refusal reasons + any micro-task completions
- **Observation window:** 2 weeks
- **Minimum resources:** 15–20 h + ≤€100
- **Proposed decision rule:** Clear objection clusters → narrow scope; diffuse or “not now” → defer
- **Inconclusive result means:** Low response → treat as weak demand signal
- **Consent/privacy:** Proportionate; genuine offers only.

### Experiment 3: Legal & delivery-capacity dry-run
- **Maps to assumptions:** 2 & 4
- **Recruitment:** 1–2 university practice offices + 2–3 target students
- **Hypothesis:** A prácticas or autónomo path can be documented for one pilot campaign within existing capacity
- **Method:** Obtain written university policy + counsel note on SS/tax/IP/GDPR; simulate team roster + hours
- **Observable action:** Written feasibility note + capacity confirmation
- **Observation window:** 2 weeks
- **Minimum resources:** 8–12 h + counsel fee (€200–500)
- **Proposed decision rule:** Operable path + capacity → proceed with any secured projects; blockers → defer or restructure
- **Inconclusive result means:** University non-response → treat as risk
- **Consent/privacy:** Proportionate; no deceptive commitments.
