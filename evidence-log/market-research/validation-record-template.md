## V1 interview / walkthrough script

1. What was the most recent application or situation that made you need a
   project example? When was it, and what exactly was requested?
2. What did you do next? Reconstruct the sequence through the actual outcome,
   including doing nothing new if that is what happened.
3. Which options did you seriously consider, which could you actually access,
   and which did you choose? Why did the chosen option win at that time?
4. If comfortable, show the relevant work, application, activity history or
   decision record. What did you personally do, who helped and who reviewed it?
5. What effort, waiting, expenses or foregone activity did this involve? Which
   parts are logged and which are retrospective estimates?
6. Where did you need clarification, access, technical help or rework? What
   worked well enough that you would keep it? What happened on another attempt,
   if there was one?
7. What did you end up showing, and what response did you actually receive?
   Separate an outcome from an expectation about what an employer might think.

Only after unaided recall, ask whether they know or have tried relevant named
alternatives. Label prompted recognition separately. Awareness is not usage.
Any response to a later hypothetical SkillBridge description is a stated
preference, not an observed switch.
