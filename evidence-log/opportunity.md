# Opportunity

**SkillBridge v2: challenge platform. 2026-10-12.**
Replaces v1 (paid projects for small businesses), kept in the
[v1 archive](archive/v1-paid-projects/opportunity.md) with the
[reasons for the pivot](archive/v1-paid-projects/README.md).

Tags: **Evidence** (linked), **Decision** (team), **Hypothesis** (untested).

## Business idea (one line)

SkillBridge lets tech students solve short, real challenges set by SaaS, engineering and data companies, and turns each result into a company-calibrated score with a verified record of what each student contributed.

## PAIN POINT

A tech student applying for internships is asked to show and explain work
beyond coursework, and struggles to prove what they personally did.
**Evidence:** 14/17 survey respondents chose "explaining my personal
contribution" and 12/17 "showing experience beyond coursework"
([survey summary](validation/survey-summary-2026-10-12.md)); 9/11 for each in
the [pilot](market-research/pilot-assessment-2026-10-01.md); interview accounts
UV-S01 and UV-S02 ([interviews](validation/interviews-2026-09-22.md)).
The survey is unscreened — year, programme and university were not collected
— and its overlap with the pilot sample is unknown, so the two counts are
reported side by side, never added
([participant fit](recruitment.md#participant-fit-against-the-target-user)).

## USER

**Student (unchanged from v1):** a 2nd–4th-year software, web or data student
whose experience is mainly coursework. 9/17 report a latest project reviewed
only by classmates or staff. **Evidence** (survey).

**Company (new):** a SaaS, engineering or data company that hires interns or
juniors and wants a better screen than a CV. **Partial interview evidence:**
V2-E01 proposes a CSV-validation challenge and review criteria; V2-R01 reports
screening and take-home review effort. Brief delivery, company payment and
repeat participation remain hypotheses.

**Recruiter (new, decides credential value):** the hiring manager or recruiter
who reads the score. Modelled in the [Skeptical Verifier](personas/ai-persona-03.md).
**Partial interview evidence:** V2-R01 favours a mock profile with inspectable
work and engineer feedback, while discounting an unexplained score. The
[26 October intake](validation/recruiter-comparison-findings-2026-10-26.md)
adds five favourable stated comparisons using the same illustrative profiles,
with reviewer, rubric and authorship conditions. The 27 October update accepts
all five dates according to availability and preserves both CVs. Screening and
the missing full rubric/work/feedback in the PDF remain gaps; no actual hiring
effect is established.

## NEED

Students need a short, bounded way to produce work that an employer will believe,
with their own contribution visible. Realistic capacity is short: 12/17 can give
four hours a week or less, none eight or more. Students want clear scope and
deadlines (11/17) and feedback (8/17 named its absence as a blocker).
**Evidence** (survey).

## INSIGHT

**Individual-contribution verification** means checking what each student
personally produced within a team. SkillBridge proposes to attach that verified
work to a company-calibrated challenge certificate. The reviewed public offers
of Forage, HackerRank certifications, Devpost and Kaggle do not describe that
combined feature. **NUWE: not publicly disclosed** in the reviewed material.
Riipen has partial overlap, so contribution verification alone is not an
established unique feature. See the dated [feature comparison](market-research/contribution-verification-review-2026-10-13.md).

**Evidence:** public feature descriptions and the student contribution problem.
**Hypothesis:** our particular certificate is more useful to employers.
V2-R01 supports inspectable evidence in one interview; this does not establish
reliable attribution by SkillBridge or market-wide exclusivity.

## HOW MIGHT WE

How might we turn a few hours of student work on a real company brief into a
result an employer trusts, with each student's contribution visible?

## Combined sentence

A 2nd–4th-year tech student with mainly coursework experience needs a short,
real piece of work an employer will believe, with their own contribution
provable, because applications ask for exactly that and coursework cannot show
it. SkillBridge's proposed difference is a short company challenge with a
certificate that makes each student's verified work inspectable; whether this
improves employer decisions over existing alternatives remains to be tested.

## Why we pivoted

v1's riskiest assumption, small-business trust in paid student work, never
reached its test: no formal pitch, charter or deposit was recorded, and one of
two business interviews had no current project. Meanwhile the student-side
evidence kept pointing to proof of contribution, short time boxes and feedback.
v2 keeps the student and the Job and changes the solution and the paying side.
Details: [archive README](archive/v1-paid-projects/README.md). **Decision** on
existing evidence. The [October interview intake](validation/v2-findings-2026-10-12.md)
adds initial concept feedback; v2 completion and paid conversion remain untested.

## Open gaps

- Five new recruiter-comparison records add concept evidence to earlier v2
  interviews. Complete screening and reconcile the saved CVs with the rubric
  and feedback mentioned in answers; student payment and actual hiring remain unmeasured.
- V2-S01 states willingness to pay EUR 15 for specific engineer-written
  feedback after completing the work. No actual payment or challenge
  submission is recorded. Feedback for every certified result is decided;
  actual delivery capacity remains unmeasured.
- The engineer's written brief, sample files and rubric sign-off are pending;
  the recruiter's conditional interest is not a company purchase.
- Survey respondents' university and programme are not recorded.
- The Madrid student-pool estimate still applies; revenue is not estimated for v2
  ([analysis](market-research/analysis.md)).
