## Stage A: Market baseline: confirmed 2026-10-06 (provisional)

**Strongest current alternative:** CA6, IE × Extern remote externship.

who used it; nothing is recorded). Chosen "for now".

### Competitive factors and trade-offs

| Factor | What alternatives assume or provide | Trade-off, contradiction or gap |
|---|---|---|
| Time commitment | Extern 10–15 h/week for 6–12 weeks; Coursera 2–5 h/week | Pilot students stated capacity of 2–7 h/week; none said 8+ (n=11, stated) |
| Access path | Apply, wait for a cohort window, be selected (Extern, Riipen via programme); Forage/Coursera open | Waiting and selection cost time |
| Flexibility (**benefit**, user view) | Asynchronous work fits around other commitments | Tension with the need for someone to turn to when stuck (P017) |
| Realness of the work | Extern project or case; Forage/Coursera practice tasks; coursework has no client | Unknown for Extern (Pin 1) |
| Proof of contribution | All give a certificate or award | 9/11 cannot explain their contribution; no alternative known to record it |
| Feedback and help | Extern about 1 h/week from employer plus mentors | Async may mean thin feedback (review excerpts, unverified) |
| Cost and pay | Extern typically unpaid, cost to students unknown; Coursera subscription; Forage free | Pay chosen by 3/11 as a driver; student time still has a cost |

**Taken for granted:** an end certificate; learning/skills as the headline promise.
