**Version:** 0.5 — 2026-10-12 (v2 interview intake)

**Status:** The Job is unchanged from v0.3. This version adds the user's
student, recruiter and engineer interviews from 2026-10-05, 2026-10-06 and

## Job to Be Done

When a tech student with mainly coursework experience applies for internships
and is asked for project examples or something they have delivered, they want
practical work they can demonstrate and explain to an employer.

**Relevant progress dimensions:** Functional: produce work that can be shown and
explained in applications. Emotional: confidence that work is good enough
(UV-S01/S02; V2-S01 Q5) and concern about a poor public score (V2-S01 Q8).
These are participant reports, not measured outcomes from using SkillBridge.

## Contradictions and validation gaps

- Survey Q5 "payment amount" (3/17) meant students **being paid** in v1; it says
  nothing about students paying. V2-S01 Q10 adds conditional willingness to pay
  EUR 15 for specific human feedback; no SkillBridge payment is recorded.
- The pilot reported students accepting v1 campaigns (P026). Whether the same
  students would choose a challenge instead is unknown.
- V2-R01 Q6-Q7 supplies one supportive mock-profile judgment, with human
  feedback and rubric provenance valued above the raw score. The exact shown
  profiles and real hiring effects are not supplied; the five-recruiter test
  remains incomplete.
- The student's payment condition assumes personal engineer-written feedback.
  The team decided on 2026-10-12 that every certified result gets it
  ([v2 findings](validation/v2-findings-2026-10-12.md#consequences-for-the-current-offer));
  whether engineer time scales beyond the pilot is open.
- Survey respondents' university, year and programme are not recorded. V2-S01
  is identified as an IE CSAI student; year and internship history are missing.
- See the [v2 findings](validation/v2-findings-2026-10-12.md) for source limits,
  company-readiness evidence and untested outcomes.
