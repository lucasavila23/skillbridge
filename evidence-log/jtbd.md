# Jobs to Be Done hypothesis

**Version:** 0.4 — 2026-10-12 (v2 challenge platform)

**Status:** The Job is unchanged from v0.3. It was written solution-neutral, so
the pivot changes only the solution hired and the switching forces. v0.3 is in
the [v1 archive](archive/v1-paid-projects/jtbd.md).

## Job to Be Done

When a tech student with mainly coursework experience applies for internships
and is asked for project examples or something they have delivered, they want
practical work they can demonstrate and explain to an employer.

## Working definitions and evidence status

Source IDs: September [interview records](validation/interviews-2026-09-22.md)
(UV-), [pilot findings](validation/pilot-findings-source-2026-10-01.md) (P/T),
and the [survey summary](validation/survey-summary-2026-10-12.md) (n = 17,
probably overlapping the 11-person pilot survey). The challenge-platform rows
are **Hypothesis**: no student has been asked about challenges.

| Lens | Element | Working definition | Basis or status |
|---|---|---|---|
| How the job begins | Circumstance | A tech student applies for internships with coursework and student projects as their main examples. | UV-S01/S02; pilot P003. Survey Q3: 9/17 latest project reviewed only by class or staff. |
| How the job begins | Trigger | An application asks for project examples or an account of something delivered, exposing uncertainty about what to show. | UV-S01/S02 Q1; pilot P016 (recruiter questions about personal work and verification). |
| Progress | Desired progress | Have practical work they can demonstrate and explain to an employer. | Survey Q2: 14/17 explaining personal contribution, 12/17 showing experience beyond coursework. Pilot T007: 9/11 each. |
| Progress | Solution hired today | Coursework and personal projects; some students try employer-branded simulations or competitions. | Coursework: survey Q3, pilot P014. Forage, HackerRank, Devpost, Kaggle: published offers only, **no use by our students evidenced** ([competitive analysis](market-research/competitive-analysis.md#v2-alternatives-challenge-and-assessment-platforms-2026-10-12)). |
| Forces compelling change | Push | Coursework does not show contribution or outside acceptance; requirements are unclear and feedback is missing. | Survey Q4: 8/17 unclear requirements, 8/17 lack of feedback, 7/17 losing motivation. Pilot P009: 8/11 latest projects had no external user. |
| Forces compelling change | Pull | A short, real brief with a score and feedback they can show, and their own contribution on record. | Survey Q5: clear scope and deadlines 11/17, career relevance 11/17, portfolio 8/17, guidance 7/17. That a **company-calibrated score** is the pull: Hypothesis. |
| Forces opposing change | Habit | Coursework and free platforms are familiar, immediate and cost nothing. | Pilot P014, P017. Every alternative reviewed is free to students. |
| Forces opposing change | Anxiety | Paying for an unproven certificate; time on top of studies; teammates free-riding on a team score; a low score being visible. | Time: survey Q6, 12/17 at four hours or less, 0 at eight or more. Payment, free-riding and score visibility: Hypothesis. |

**Relevant progress dimensions:** Functional: produce work that can be shown and
explained in applications. Emotional: confidence that the work is "good enough"
for a real company (UV-S01/S02 uncertainty; Hypothesis beyond that).

## Contradictions and validation gaps

- Survey Q5 "payment amount" (3/17) meant students **being paid** in v1; it says
  nothing about students paying. Willingness to pay is untested.
- The pilot reported students accepting v1 campaigns (P026). Whether the same
  students would choose a challenge instead is unknown.
- No recruiter has been asked whether a challenge score changes a screening
  decision.
- Survey respondents' university, year and programme are not recorded.
