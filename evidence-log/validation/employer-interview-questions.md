# Large-employer interview questions

## 1. Hiring situation and missing evidence

- Think about the last internship recruitment round you were involved in.
  What did you ask students to show, and what made it difficult to decide who
  should progress?
- Can you describe a particular application where that happened, without
  identifying the candidate?

## 2. Practical experience and its impact

- Where, if anywhere, have recent interns needed more support than you
  expected?
- What happened, and what did that require from your team?

## 3. Current assessment and training

- How do you currently assess or develop those skills?
- What time or money do you spend on that, and what still doesn't work well?

## 4. Small projects as evidence for hiring

- Suppose an applicant had delivered a small website or dashboard for a
  business. How, if at all, would that affect your assessment?
- What would you need to see?
- What would make that experience irrelevant or unconvincing?

## 5. Prototype walkthrough

- As you explore this illustrative prototype, with template student profiles
  and simulated signing and payment actions, can you talk through what you
  think each step is asking you to do?
- **Project offer:** What would completing this project demonstrate about a
  student? What would it leave untested?
- **Student profile templates:** What would you inspect first? What
  information is missing?
- **Delivery plan:** Which parts, if any, would develop skills relevant to
  your interns?
- **Charter and simulated deposit:** What do you understand each party is
  committing to? What would concern you?
- **Separate proposed project-record outline (concept only):** If this
  appeared in an internship application, what would you inspect, and what
  decision would it help you make?

## 6. Reason and budget to pay

- Would your organisation have a reason to fund this kind of experience,
  for example by sponsoring a student project, or would its value to you
  mainly be in reviewing applicants afterwards? Why?
- If you identify a reason to fund it, which budget would cover that?
- Who approves that spending, and what comparable activity have you funded
  before?

## 7. Priced pilot and payment decision

- Based on the written pilot offer at [total price], with [deposit amount]
  upfront and the stated scope, responsibilities, completion deadline, and
  refund terms, would you like to proceed, propose a change, or decline?
- What determines your decision?
- If you choose to proceed with a live pilot, can we agree who will sign
  the actual agreement and the date by which the real deposit will be
  transferred?
