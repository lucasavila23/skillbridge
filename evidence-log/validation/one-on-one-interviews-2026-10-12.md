## OO-S01: recent graduate

1. **Would you use SkillBridge? Why or why not?** "Yes, but only if the platform is credible and its certifications are recognized by major companies and recruiters."
2. **Would you pay €15 for certification?** "Yes, if the certification is officially recognized by reputable companies and adds value to my CV."
3. **If not €15, how much would you pay?** "€5–10 if the platform is relatively new, but up to €15 if it is recognized by major employers."
4. **How many hours per week?** "Around 3–5 hours, depending on my university workload."
5. **Alone or in a team?** "Both, as individual challenges demonstrate personal skills, while teamwork develops collaboration skills."
6. **Who should pay for certification?** "Ideally, companies, as they benefit from identifying skilled candidates. However, students could pay a small fee if the certification has proven value."

## OO-S02: current IE student

1. **Would you use a platform like SkillBridge?** "Yes. Real company challenges give me proof of what I can actually do, which a CV alone doesn't. It's also a way to stand out when I have little formal work experience."
2. **Would you pay €15 to certify your score?** "Yes, if the certification is recognized by recruiters and the challenge was from a real company. €15 is reasonable as a one-off fee."
3. **If not €15, how much?** "€15 works for me. I'd probably stop at €20-25 unless it clearly leads to interviews."
4. **Hours per week?** "About 4-6 hours per week alongside classes and other commitments."
5. **Alone or in a team?** "A mix. Team challenges are closer to real work, but solo ones make it clearer what I personally contributed. The platform should track individual contribution either way."
6. **Who should pay: students or companies?** "Ideally companies, since they get access to vetted talent and save on hiring costs. Students should only pay a small fee, if any, so cost doesn't shut people out."

## OO-R01: recruiter

1. **Would you trust scores from real company challenges?** "Yes, more than grades or a CV, because they show applied skills on realistic problems. Trust depends on how the scoring is done."
2. **Would verified individual contribution make you more likely to interview?** "Yes. In team projects it's hard to know who did what, so verified individual contribution reduces that doubt and makes me more likely to interview."
3. **What evidence would you need to trust the scores?** "A clear scoring rubric, review by the company that set the challenge, the actual work sample, and consistency across candidates. Anti-cheating checks and some benchmark of how others scored would help too."
4. **Who should pay for the platform?** "Companies, since they benefit directly from a pre-screened talent pool. A small student fee for certification is acceptable."
