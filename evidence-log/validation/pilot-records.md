## Arm B: 15-minute recruiter comparison

Recruit more than five if needed to obtain five complete, qualifying records.
Use the first five qualifying completed comparisons in chronological order;
log refusals, incomplete sessions and exclusions with reasons. Do not replace
a negative answer. Additional comparisons are a separate exploratory sample.

**Screen:** “Have you personally screened software, web or data interns or
juniors in the last 12 months? What role and roughly when?” Save the answer.

**Opening:** “We're testing whether this evidence would be useful in junior
hiring. These are two versions of the same candidate's profile. [Identify any
mock content.] There's no right answer; neither or no difference is useful
feedback too. May we retain anonymised notes?”

1. “Think of your last relevant hiring round. What did you require before
   shortlisting, and where was an applicant's individual work hard to judge?”
2. Show the saved comparison pack, alternating which version appears on the
   left across sessions. Give the same factual pass/fail explanation: students
   must pass and verify authorship before paying; payment cannot change a
   score; the certificate includes engineer feedback. Avoid a sales pitch.
3. “For that role, would you shortlist the CV-only version: yes, no, or unsure?
   And the version with the certified result: yes, no, or unsure? Why?”
4. “Does the attached evidence change or support that shortlisting decision,
   have no effect, or make you less confident? Which part drives that?”
5. “What, if anything, is missing before you would use it? What would make
   you distrust it?”
6. “Could this replace any part of your current take-home assessment? Which
   part, and what would you still require?”

**Counting rule for the existing B metric:** count a positive only when the
respondent explicitly says the bundle shown changes or supports shortlisting
and identifies the relevant evidence. Both versions can be shortlisted if
the respondent explicitly says the bundle supports the decision. Mere liking,
an ambiguous answer, or support contingent on an absent feature is not a
positive. Retain those answers and conditions in the denominator. Report the
observed mock-profile choices separately; this is stated judgment, not hiring
uplift. Fewer than five usable qualifying records is inconclusive.
