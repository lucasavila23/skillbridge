## Reconcile the PDF with the interview notes

The [source introduction](sources/2026-10-26-skillbridge-cv-test-interviews.md)
explains that passing and authorship verification precede payment, payment cannot
change a score, and certification includes engineer feedback. These rules were
described in the interview script; they are not printed on the supplied CVs.

All five Q3 answers refer to engineer comments or written feedback; R01 Q2 also
refers to inspecting code, and R02/R05 refer to a rubric breakdown. Those items
are not present in this PDF. The supplied record does not establish whether
additional materials were displayed, described orally, or assumed by respondents.
Preserve the answers unchanged and resolve that distinction with the interviewers.
Do not create missing historical feedback, code or rubrics after the fact.

- **Closed gap:** the two CV versions are retained and their common baseline
  can be inspected. All five respondents reportedly saw this same pair.
- **Open gap:** the full bundle required by Arm B is not evidenced by the PDF.
  Recover any additional materials actually shown; otherwise report this as a
  CV-plus-description concept comparison and precommit a separate full-bundle
  test. Any new materials get a new version and new results.
- **Trust limits:** fictional company/reviewer claims do not establish real
  endorsement, reliable authorship checks or independent scoring. Pass/fail
  rates and acceptance of AI-assisted grading also remain untested.
