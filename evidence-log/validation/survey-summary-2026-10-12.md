# Student survey summary (2026-10-12)

**Source:** [survey-responses.csv](survey-responses.csv), pulled from the live
published Google Sheet with [pull-survey.sh](pull-survey.sh) on 2026-10-12.
**n = 17** responses, timestamps from 2026-09-22. Self-reported, multi-select
where noted. Q2 allowed up to three choices,
but 3 respondents picked four or five; counts include every selection as given. Respondents' university, programme and screening are not recorded
in the sheet; the [pilot assessment](../market-research/pilot-assessment-2026-10-01.md)
(n = 11) probably overlaps with these responses and must not be added to them.

| Question | Result (n = 17) |
|---|---|
| Q1 Application outcome, last 12 months | 6 applied, no interview · 4 waiting · 3 interview, no offer · 2 at least one offer · 2 not applied |
| Q2 Difficulties (up to 3) | **14 explaining personal contribution** · **12 showing experience beyond coursework** · 8 finding opportunities · 6 technical requirements · 6 interviews or technical assessments · 2 time |
| Q3 Most recent project outside a job | 9 coursework reviewed only by class/staff · 5 for a business or client · 2 personal, unused · 1 used by outsiders |
| Q4 What made completion hard (up to 2) | 8 unclear requirements · 8 lack of feedback or guidance · 7 losing motivation · 5 lack of time · 4 skill gaps · 4 teammate coordination |
| Q5 What would influence participation (up to 3) | 11 clear scope and deadlines · 11 career relevance · 8 portfolio-showable · 7 technical guidance · 5 feedback or reference from the business · **3 payment amount** |
| Q6 Weekly time, next four weeks | 9 two–four hours · 5 five–seven hours · 3 under two hours · **0 eight or more** |
| Q7 Next step if a relevant project appeared | 7 short info session · 6 review a brief, payment and schedule · 3 nothing now · 1 occasional updates |

## What it supports and what it does not

- **Supports:** proving individual contribution and experience beyond coursework
  are the most common application difficulties; students want clear scope,
  deadlines and feedback; realistic capacity is short (12/17 at four hours or less).
- **Does not show:** willingness to pay for anything (never asked), how employers
  read any credential, or actual participation (Q7 is stated intention).
- The survey was written for the v1 paid-project model; Q5 "payment amount"
  means students being paid, not paying.
