## UV-B01

**Participant:** Owner of Baya coffee shop, as identified by the user.

1. **Recent digital need**

   > “We changed some prices, and customers were still finding an old menu online. We needed to update it because staff kept having to explain the difference.”

2. **Current alternative**

   > “One of our employees updated the Instagram posts. A friend originally made the website, so we messaged him about that part.”

**Classification:** Questions 1–4 and the existing relationship in Question 5
are participant reports. Conditions for a new provider and Question 6 are
stated preferences or intentions. The described menu work is a past episode;
an open project now available for a student team has not been confirmed.
No agreed price, signed charter or transferred deposit was supplied.

has a current project worth outsourcing, not a failed formal pitch.
