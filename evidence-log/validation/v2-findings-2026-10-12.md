## Consequences for the current offer

Decided by the team on 2026-10-12 in response to this intake, with the
certification rule revised on 2026-10-13 as noted below. Price, experiment
thresholds and the pilot window are unchanged; these settle offer details
that the interviews showed were ambiguous.

1. **Decided: every paying student receives engineer-written feedback in the
   pilot.** The interviews exposed a conflict between the concept shown to
   V2-S01 (engineer reviews top submissions only) and the payment condition
   they stated (personal, human-written feedback). At the Arm A threshold
   — 10 submitting students, 30% paying — that is 3 individual certified
   results, each requiring feedback. **Workload clarified 2026-10-14:** V2-E01's
   one hour covered reviewing three solutions, not the whole pilot. For ten
   solo submissions, the current five-review sample alone is estimated at
   100 minutes, with setup, extra eligibility work, feedback, debriefs and
   coordination also counted. Reuse overlapping work once; shared team review
   does not replace each student's feedback. See the
   [cost accounting](../ERRC.md#engineer-workload-and-cost). Capacity and
   scalability remain open risks.
2. **Decided: the certified result is an evidence bundle, not a score.** The
   recruiter's useful signals are work, contribution, rubric provenance and
   engineer commentary, and V2-R01 would skip an unexplained 82/100. The
   certified result and the Arm B mock profile therefore show all four, never
   a bare number. Score calibration and the authenticity check remain open.
3. **Revised decision, 2026-10-13: certification can be failed.** Results stay
   private by default, the student chooses what to share, and any certificate
   shows the actual score. The earlier decision allowed certification at any
   score; the team has replaced that rule. Only students meeting the published
   challenge requirements and individual-work verification can receive the
   €15 certification offer. Failed results receive no certificate and incur no
   certification fee. This addresses V2-S01's concern about buying a pass as a
   design decision; its credibility is still to be tested with recruiters.
   The challenge-specific pass mark and checks must be agreed before the pilot:
   [current eligibility rules](../experiment-card.md#certification-eligibility).
4. **Decided: the CSV proposal becomes a written brief by 2026-10-20**
   (owner: Lucas Avila). Confirm with the engineer the examples, scope, scoring
   weights, review deadline, feedback coverage (settled by decision 1) and
   permitted use of submissions — V2-E01 Q5 expects students to retain their
   code and the task to be used for assessment only. The PDF's closing
   request still has no recorded answer, so nothing in it authorises outreach
   or establishes sign-off; the brief and rubric must be signed separately.
   If neither exists by that date, the experiment card's stop condition runs
   Arm B alone.
