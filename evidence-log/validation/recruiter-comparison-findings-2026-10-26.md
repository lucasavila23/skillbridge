## Findings and consequences

| Finding | Evidence | Current implication / next check |
|---|---|---|
| Full evidence is more persuasive than a bare score | R01/R04 Q3 explicitly discount numbers alone; all Q3 answers name work, contribution, rubric or engineer feedback | Retain the existing full-bundle direction. Do not infer the isolated effect of a single component. |
| Reviewer provenance is part of the trust requirement | R01/R04 Q4 | Next evidence pack should make the reviewer's role/credentials and the rubric's provenance inspectable. Do not label a hypothetical reviewer as verified. |
| Authorship checks must be explained, not just asserted | R03 Q4; R01/R03 Q3 | Show the actual individual-check method and its limits; test whether students can explain their work. Contribution logs alone are not proven reliable attribution. |
| Passing must remain independent of payment | R02/R05 Q4 | Retain earned pass/fail certification. R02 also wants pass/fail distributions; collect real results before publishing rates. |
| AI efficiency and recruiter credibility may conflict | R04 Q4 objects to automated/generic grading | Explain automated first-pass scoring versus engineer review and test whether that division is acceptable. This interview does not validate the AI-assisted scoring model. |
| Replacement value is conditional and varies by employer | All Q5 answers; different live checks retained | Test one real screening use with a confirmed employer and measure actual reviewer effort. Do not promise measured savings or full interview replacement. |

These findings strengthen the **Skeptical Verifier** persona and the rationale
for the evidence bundle. They do not change the student JTBD, product goal,
€15 test price, revenue ranking or the 30% / 3-of-5 thresholds. Changes to the
next comparison pack must be versioned and reported separately, not substituted
for the materials used in these five interviews.
