01 / Challenge from an example data company

Flag faulty sensor readings before they reach the dashboard

Northwind Analytics (illustrative SaaS company) streams temperature readings from 2,000 warehouse sensors. Some sensors drift or freeze. Build a check that flags bad readings and explain your approach to their engineering team.

What you deliver

  • A script or notebook that labels each reading as OK, drifting or frozen
  • Results on the provided 7-day sample (CSV, 140,000 rows)
  • A one-page README: approach, trade-offs, what you would do next
  • For teams: a task list or commit history showing who did what

How it is scored

Rubric criterionWeight
Detection quality on hidden test readings (automated)35%
Code clarity and structure20%
Reasoning and trade-offs in the README20%
Handling of edge cases (gaps, duplicates, time zones)15%
Communication: could the team ship this?10%

Rubric signed off by a Northwind data engineer. Automated tests and an AI rubric score every submission; the engineer reviews the top submissions and any score the AI flags as uncertain.
