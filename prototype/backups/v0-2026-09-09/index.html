01 / An example café project

A three-page café website

A student team helps customers find the café, browse its menu, and plan a visit. The business gets a completed website; students get real client experience.

What the team would deliver

Outside this example's scope

Online ordering, payments, bookings, new branding, ongoing maintenance, and hosting or domain fees.

Menu text, approved images, contact details, and the website access agreed at kickoff.

03 / Working together

A clear plan for the student team

Agree the work and completion deadline before it starts. Keep the business involved through brief, structured meetings.

  1. 01

    Agree the charter and deposit

    Sign the project charter and transfer 10% of the agreed total within ten days of receiving the pitch.

  2. 02

    Confirm the team and kick off

    Within five business days of a decision to continue with delivery, confirm the student team match, introduce the team by email or phone, and deliver the final, mutually signed schedule.

  3. 03

    15-minute weekly sync

    The business and student team review progress, answer questions, and clarify the files or account access needed for the work.

  4. 04

    30-minute final evaluation

    The team presents the agreed deliverables. If satisfied, the business provides a written testimonial or LinkedIn recommendation.

Kickoff and completion are separate

The five-business-day milestone covers the match, introduction, and signed schedule.

Project delivery follows the completion deadline in the charter.

Facilitator notes and live-test readiness

This v0 makes the risky commitment step inspectable. It uses an illustrative website project, a sample quote, and empty student profile templates.

Before live pitches: document the pricing method, collect actual available student profiles and coursework evidence, agree each real project's scope and deadline, and prepare the actual charter and payment route. Identify the qualifying businesses in the recruitment log.

Live metric: signed charter plus verified 10% transfer within ten days. Keep all ten pitched businesses in the denominator, including non-responses.

Decision rule: 2+ conversions → recruit students, form teams, and kick off; 0 → the separate $10 registration / one-week micro-task test; exactly 1 → the separate five-pitch extension. With no extension conversion, fulfill the single project and stop scaling. With 1+, halt outreach and form teams for the secured projects.
