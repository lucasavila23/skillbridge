# SkillBridge rough test — v0

## Purpose

Make the business owner's decision to entrust a real, paid project to a student
team inspectable. The walkthrough follows the current
[Experiment Card](../evidence-log/experiment-card.md).

This artifact supports a rehearsal and rubric review. Rehearsal choices are not
business commitments or experiment results.

## The decision to show

Review the project offer → inspect student capability → understand the delivery
plan → review the charter and 10% deposit → proceed, ask a question, or decline.

| Part | What the business owner needs to inspect |
|---|---|
| Project offer | Business need, outcome, deliverables, scope boundaries, completion deadline, agreed total price, and how the documented pricing method produced that quote. |
| Student capability | One-page profiles from actual, available students: relevant coursework, evidence of completed coursework projects, skills, and a short motivation statement. Profiles represent the available pool; final teams are formed and matched after the deposit. |
| Delivery plan | A student team delivers the project. Within five business days of a decision to continue, confirm the team match, introduce the team to the business, and deliver the mutually signed schedule. Include a 15-minute weekly sync and a 30-minute final evaluation. |
| Commitment | Inspect the project charter and deposit amount. Both signature and transfer are required within ten days of the pitch. The deposit is 10% of that project's agreed total price, credited toward that price and refundable if the agreed completion deadline is missed. |

Project scopes and total prices may vary. Define the pricing method before the
first pitch and retain it, the deposit percentage, and commitment conditions for
all ten primary pitches and any five-pitch extension.

## Two-minute rehearsal

1. **0:00–0:25 — Offer:** explain the business outcome and inspect the scope and quote.
2. **0:25–0:55 — Capability:** inspect the student profile format and coursework evidence.
3. **0:55–1:20 — Delivery:** explain team formation, kickoff, meetings, and completion.
4. **1:20–2:00 — Decision:** inspect the charter and deposit terms; demonstrate proceeding or declining.

Observe where the reviewer hesitates or asks for missing information. Record
their actual words and what they inspected. Keep rehearsal feedback separate
from live conversion evidence.

## Inputs required for live pitches

- A documented, consistently applied pricing method.
- A real project brief, scope, quote, and completion deadline for each business.
- Actual available student profiles, coursework examples, and permission to use them.
- A usable project charter and a real way to transfer and verify the deposit.
- Access to qualifying operational businesses through their owners or general managers,
  recorded in [recruitment.md](../evidence-log/recruitment.md).

Missing inputs must remain visibly marked as placeholders in the rehearsal.
Illustrative projects or amounts must be labelled as examples, and empty profile
templates must not be presented as real students.

## Live evidence and decision

For each pitched business, record a unique identifier, qualification and
decision-maker role, pitch date and response deadline, offered scope and total
price, pricing-method reference, deposit amount, signature evidence, transfer
evidence, and any stated reason for declining. Use an access-controlled location
for identifiable participant details and payment records.

Keep all ten primary businesses in the denominator, including non-responses.
Count a conversion only when both the charter signature and 10% transfer occur
within that business's ten-day window.

- **2 or more out of 10:** continue to student recruitment, team formation, and kickoff.
- **0 out of 10:** follow the separate $10 registration-fee / one-week micro-task test.
- **Exactly 1 out of 10:** run the separate, precommitted five-pitch extension.
  The original result stays below threshold. With no additional conversion,
  fulfill the single project and stop scaling; with at least one additional
  conversion, stop outreach and form teams for the secured projects.

The complete conditions and next actions remain in the Experiment Card. No live
outreach or conversion evidence has been recorded in this artifact.
